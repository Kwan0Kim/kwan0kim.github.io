About Me

I’m Kwanyeong Kim, and I’m currently seeking a Ph.D. program. I earned B.S. degrees in Astronomy and Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University in 2024. I went on to complete my M.S. in Astronomy at Yonsei in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Chandeok Park, whose lab I had joined as an undergraduate.

I’m interested in optimal guidance and control for spacecraft. My master’s thesis used physics-informed neural networks to study finite-horizon optimal feedback control. My broader interests include Hamilton–Jacobi theory and learning-based methods for astrodynamics. You can read about my projects on the Research page.
