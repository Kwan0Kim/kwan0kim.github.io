About Me.

Background

I am a researcher in the Department of Astronomy at Yonsei University. My research interests include optimal guidance and control, automatic control, astrodynamics, Hamilton–Jacobi theory, and deep learning.

I have worked in the Navigation, Control & Design for Autonomous Systems Laboratory under Prof. Chandeok Park since 2022, first as an undergraduate researcher and then as a graduate research assistant.
